The domain requires x>1 and a-x>0, so a>1 and 1<x<a.
Combining the logarithms gives (x-1)(a-x)=2.
The product is maximal at x=(a+1)/2 and its maximum is (a-1)^2/4.
Thus a<1+2sqrt(2): no solutions; equality: one solution x=1+sqrt(2); greater: two solutions x=(a+1 +/- sqrt((a-1)^2-8))/2.
At a=4, the roots are x=2 and x=3. Check both in the original logarithms.
The distance between the two roots is sqrt((a-1)^2-8). It equals 1 only when a=4, which is in the two-root range.
